Chaukanna listens to both sides of a phone call. Every few seconds of speech is transcribed and judged by the classifier (a keyword fast path, then Jev). When the call turns into a scam, it whispers a warning only the victim can hear and hands the incident to n8n, which alerts the family and drafts the 1930 complaint. This console replays every call chunk by chunk, or follows one live as it happens.
